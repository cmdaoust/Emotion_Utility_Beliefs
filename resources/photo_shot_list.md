## Tips
- Use the actual lab kit (SS2L and SS57L leads, our electrodes).
- Plain background; shoot close-ups from above with even light.
- Landscape for full-slide images; roughly square for photos that sit next to text.
- No participant photos. Crop faces out of any torso shots.
- Screenshots: Win + Shift + S captures part of the screen. Circle the thing to click in red, like the MP36 switch photo.
- Diagram fix: the bottom-right dot in `ECG_Fig_ElectrodePlacement.png` is grey. Plain black would vanish on the black silhouette, so use black with a white outline.
