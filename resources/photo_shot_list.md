## ECG (participant slideshow)
- [ ] **ECG electrode, gel side**: so participants know which way down it goes
- [ ] **Electrodes as you hand them over**: 3 gelled electrodes with the backing off, gel side up
- [ ] **ECG lead laid out flat**: white, black and red wires, the joining clip and the crocodile clip
- [ ] **Electrode being placed**: held by the edge, gel side down, stud facing out
- [ ] **Lead clip close-up**: the two side tabs and the metal side (one photo from each side)
- [ ] **Clip being attached**: tabs squeezed, lined up over the stud (or a short video for the "How the clips work" slide)
- [ ] **Clip attached correctly**: flat and fully on the stud
- [ ] **Clip attached wrongly** (optional): half on, or the wrong way round, for a right-vs-wrong comparison
- [ ] **White lead fed under a top**: the wire going up under the hem to the collarbone
- [ ] **Crocodile clip on a waistband**: wires hanging loose, not pulling
- [ ] **Each finished position on a torso**: collarbone, bottom right ribs, bottom left ribs (consenting colleague in a vest, or a mannequin; no face)
- [ ] **Full set-up in the mirror**: taken from where the participant sits
- [ ] **Participant's desk**: mirror and kit laid out, as they'll see it

## EDA (checklist and training slides)
- [ ] **Taping the EDA electrodes**: a small strip of medical tape over each patch and around the back of the finger. Ideally two photos, palm side and back of the hand, so students can see where the tape goes.
- [ ] **EDA electrodes on the fingertips before taping**: our own version of the placement photo (left hand, palm up, red on the index finger, black on the middle finger)

- [ ] **Medical tape roll**

## Tips
- Use the actual lab kit (SS2L and SS57L leads, our electrodes).
- Plain background; shoot close-ups from above with even light.
- Landscape for full-slide images; roughly square for photos that sit next to text.
- No participant photos. Crop faces out of any torso shots.
- Diagram fix: recolour the grey dot black in `ECG_Fig_ElectrodePlacement.png`.
