Part 1

Setting up

Stimulus PC

  1. Log in, and set the volume.
  2. Open PsychoPy from the desktop shortcut, then open the experiment file.
  3. Open the information and consent form, then the demographics form, from their desktop shortcuts.
Never edit or save the experiment on the lab PC. If PsychoPy asks to save changes, choose Don't save.

Biopac PC and MP36: order matters

  1. Check the MP36's USB and power cables at the back.
  2. Turn the MP36 on, then open the Biopac software.
  3. Open the template graph.
If the software opens before the MP36 is on, it can't find it. Close it, switch the MP36 on, and open it again.

Two electrodes, two gels

ECG electrodes (round)Peel off the backing, check the gel isn't dry, add a drop of Spectra 360 if needed. Participants get them ready to stick on.
EDA electrodesCheck the sponge, add isotonic gel if dry, and work it in with a fingertip or the syringe.
Never put Spectra 360 on an EDA electrode. It isn't made for skin conductance and changes the readings. EDA gets isotonic gel only.
Part 2

Consent, and what to say

Consent

  1. Ask them to read the information sheet and complete the consent form on screen.
  2. Wait outside while they do it.
  3. When they call you, check Qualtrics reached the end of the survey.
Don't explain the tasks or the on-screen instructions, even if asked. What participants are told is part of what's being tested. Stick to the scripts in the checklist.
Part 3

Putting on the sensors

ECG: the participant does it

  1. Show them the participant ECG slideshow (desktop shortcut: ECG Setup - EmoUtilityBelief).
  2. Hand over the 3 prepared electrodes and the three-wire lead.
  3. Leave the room. They use the mirror.
  4. If they ask for help, ask permission before touching.

EDA: what you're measuring

EDA (skin conductance) picks up tiny changes in sweat on the fingertips, which rise with emotional arousal. The signal is very small, so a good recording depends on good contact between electrode and skin.

Warm handsCold fingers give a weak, flat signal. Rub hands or rinse in lukewarm water.
Dry skinDry the fingers thoroughly before placing electrodes.
No alcoholDon't use alcohol wipes or sanitiser. They dry the skin and change the reading.

EDA: placing the electrodes

  1. Electrodes go on the left hand. Ask them to turn their palm up.
  2. One electrode on the pad of the top segment of the index finger, one on the middle finger.
  3. Centre the sponge, keep it off the creases, and press around the sticky edge.
  4. Tape each patch with a small strip of medical tape around the back of the finger. Secure but not tight.

EDA: clipping on the leads

  1. Squeeze the side tabs, metal side down, push onto the stud until it clicks.
  2. Red → index finger    Black → middle finger
  3. Lay the cable along the palm and wrist so it doesn't pull on the electrodes.
Part 4

Checking the signals

A good ECG

  • A tall, sharp spike for every beat
  • A regular rhythm
  • A fairly flat line in between
Noisy or missing? Check the wire colours are on the right electrodes and every clip has clicked on. Then try new electrodes.

EDA: what can go wrong

Flat. A lead isn't connected or an electrode isn't touching the skin.

Sudden spikes. A loose lead or poor contact.

Stepped or jumpy. Zoomed in too far, not enough gel, or dried-out electrodes.

Fix in this order: See all data → re-clip both leads → new electrodes with gel worked in.

The sniff test

  1. Once the EDA line is smooth, ask for two sharp sniffs through the nose.
  2. Within a few seconds the trace should rise, then slowly fall.
  3. No response? Go back through the troubleshooting.

Show them movement

  1. Turn the Biopac screen so they can see the traces.
  2. Ask them to tap their fingers and shift in the chair, and show them how the traces jump.
  3. Then ask them to keep their left hand still and their upper body fairly still during the experiment.
Part 5

Running and finishing

Running the experiment

  1. Keep the Biopac recording running from the signal check to the end.
  2. Demographics form first, then press Run in PsychoPy.
  3. In the start-up box, enter the participant ID and choose the loopOrder. Double-check both.
  4. Headphones on, left hand still, right hand on the mouse. Then leave the room.

Finishing

Stop and saveStop the recording and save it straight away with the participant ID.
Remove and debriefThey remove all the electrodes themselves (step outside for the ECG). Make sure they read the debrief.
Save the dataCopy the PsychoPy data to the agreed storage. Never put participant data on GitHub.
The debrief explains that the information about emotions and performance was part of the study. No participant should leave without reading it.
