ECG Self-Setup

Setting up your heart sensors

How to put on the ECG electrodes yourself

The researcher will wait outside while you do this. Just call if you need help.

What we'll be recording

An ECG records the small electrical signal your heart makes each time it beats. It is painless: the sensors only listen, and nothing is sent into your body.

1. StickPut on three sticky electrodes
2. ClipClip a coloured wire to each one
3. CheckThe researcher checks the signal on the computer
You'll do this on your own, using the mirror, while the researcher waits outside. Just call if you need help.

What you have in front of you

3 electrodesSmall round stickers with a metal stud.
3 wireswhite, black and red, joined together with a clip
Please don't touch the gel on the electrodes. It helps the sensor pick up your heartbeat.
Step 1

Stick on the electrodes

Where and how to stick them

Right
shoulder
  • Top: just under your right collarbone
  • Bottom right: bottom of your ribs, straight below the first one
  • Bottom left: bottom of your ribs on your left side
  1. Place each one sticky (white) side down, with the metal stud facing out.
  2. Press firmly around the edge of the sticker so it lies flat.
  3. Check in the mirror that all three match the diagram before you add the wires.
Step 2

Clip on the wires

How the clips work

Each wire ends in a small clip.

  1. Squeeze the two tabs on the sides of the clip.
  2. Hold it with the metal side facing down, towards the electrode.
  3. Push it onto the metal stud and let go. You should feel it click into place. A loose clip is the most common cause of a poor signal.
✓ Right

Metal side down, lying flat

✗ Wrong

Upside down, metal side up

Don't attach any clips yet. Click Next to see which wire goes on which electrode.

The wires go under your top

All three wires run under your clothes.

Next, we'll go through one wire at a time.

On the photo: VIN− = white, GND = black, VIN+ = red

White wire: right collarbone

  1. Feed the white wire up underneath your top.
  2. Clip it onto the electrode under your right collarbone.
  3. This one can be hard to see. Use the mirror, and call the researcher if you'd like help. They will always ask before touching.

  1. Clip the black wire onto the electrode at the bottom of your ribs on the right, straight below the white one.

  2. Then clip the wires' crocodile clip to the waistband of your trousers or skirt, so the wires don't pull on the electrodes.
